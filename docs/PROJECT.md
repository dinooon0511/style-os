# PELCLO — Product context

## Purpose
PELCLO is a mobile-first Russian-language men's styling assistant. Its initial audience is men approximately 25–35. The app aims to simplify wardrobe organization, outfit creation, and shopping decisions.

## Core user experiences
- Home: daily recommendations and entry points to styling actions.
- Wardrobe: user's saved clothing and adding items.
- Outfits: multiple outfit suggestions, including building around a selected item.
- Catalog: browse clothing and relevant item details.
- Profile: user information, avatar, and settings.
- Authentication and onboarding.

## Product direction and historical decisions
- Launch initially for men; free product at the initial stage.
- Use existing wardrobe items and curated styling rules.
- Support location-aware weather and daily outfit recommendations.
- Shopping recommendations may later use a curated collection of products.
- Keep Russian grammar and clothing descriptions natural.
- Focus on a simple, approachable, trustworthy experience.
